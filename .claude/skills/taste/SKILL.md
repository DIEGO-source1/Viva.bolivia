# Taste — Viva Bolivia

Use with `.claude/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/animation-craft/SKILL.md`, and `/DESIGN.md`.

## Design judgment
- Every screen needs a clear visual idea, not just a collection of components.
- Prefer a small number of strong decisions over many decorative effects.
- Use local context from Cochabamba and Bolivia in the visual language; do not rely on generic stock travel tropes.
- Let photography carry emotion while typography, spacing, and interface controls carry clarity.
- Use contrast, scale, alignment, and whitespace to establish hierarchy.
- Use asymmetry intentionally; maintain a strong underlying grid.
- Avoid template-like gradients, repeated rounded cards, arbitrary icon decoration, overused glass effects, and excessive badges.
- Choose imagery that is specific to the place and experience. Do not mislabel generic imagery as a real local attraction.
- Keep the planning task prominent and easy to complete.

## Review checklist
1. Can the user understand the page purpose in five seconds?
2. Is the first viewport memorable and specific to the valley?
3. Is there one clear primary action?
4. Are headings, labels, and body copy distinct in hierarchy?
5. Does the layout feel composed rather than mechanically tiled?
6. Do imagery and copy support one coherent story?
7. Are mobile layouts designed intentionally rather than merely compressed?
8. Can every visual effect justify its presence?
